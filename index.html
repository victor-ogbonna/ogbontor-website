03 What we do

A community, a school, and a workshop — in one place

Africa imports the technology it depends on. The capacity to build it is already here, untrained and unequipped. That is the gap we close.

We train

Nineteen modules, Ohm's law to FPGA. Taught with a board in front of you.

We build

Real prototypes from week one. Fourteen systems on the bench so far.

We incubate

Capstones that prove themselves become products. We help with IP and pilot users.

We manufacture

Hardware designed and assembled in Enugu, not imported.

07 The community

You will not be building alone

The hardest hour in hardware is the one where nothing works and you have no idea why. A community is what gets you through it.

  • Weekly build nights — open lab, people to ask.
  • Three-week cohort bootcamps — intensive, and free.
  • Mentorship — someone two steps ahead, not twenty.
  • A component library — borrow it, don't buy it.
  • Demo days — show it to people who understand it.
