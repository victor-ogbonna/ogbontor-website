Cohort bootcamps

Intensive, in person, and free

Bootcamps are how most members enter. A cohort moves through the foundational tier together over a fixed number of weeks, in the lab, with boards on the bench from day one.

You are not watching someone else build. You get a kit, a partner, a bug, and somebody experienced within arm's reach when you are stuck.

  • Cohort-based — you start and finish with the same people.
  • Hardware supplied — boards, sensors and tools from the community library.
  • Project-assessed — you pass by building, not by writing.
  • Free for members — the cost barrier is the thing we are removing.

The curriculum

Nineteen modules, in the order they actually make sense

Tiers I through IV are taught modules. Tier V is the capstone — a real build, on a deadline, in front of real users.

I

Foundational Knowledge

Core principles — the vocabulary and habits everything else is built on.

  • 01Introduction to Embedded Systems
  • 02Fundamentals of Electronics and Circuit Design
  • 03Programming Essentials for Embedded Systems
  • 04Introduction to Robotics
II

Intermediate Skills

Practical application — where members start shipping working hardware.

  • 05Microcontroller Programming and Applications
  • 06Sensors and Actuators
  • 07Communication Protocols
  • 08Real-Time Operating Systems (RTOS)
  • 09Internet of Things (IoT) Systems and Applications
III

Advanced Topics

Specialised knowledge — the work that separates a hobbyist from an engineer.

  • 10Advanced Robotics and AI Integration
  • 11Edge Computing and AI for Embedded Systems
  • 12FPGA Programming and Applications
  • 13Power Management in Embedded Systems
  • 14CPU Design and Architecture
  • 15GPU Design and Parallel Processing
  • 16Autonomous Systems and Control
  • 17Advanced Sensor Fusion Techniques
IV

CAD and Design Integration

Turning a breadboard into something you can hold, mount and manufacture.

  • 18CAD for Embedded Systems and Robotics
  • 19Design for Embedded Systems and Robotics Projects
V

Capstone Projects

Real builds, real deadlines, real users.

  • Real-world embedded systems and robotics projects with CAD design
  • Industry internships and collaborations
  • Entrepreneurship and startup development
  • Ethical considerations in embedded systems and robotics
Specialist tracks

Where members go after the core

Once the foundational and intermediate tiers are behind you, you pick a direction and go deep with a project team working on it.

Robotics & Control

Kinematics, PID, motor drivers, chassis design and the arm rig.

IoT & Connectivity

MQTT, LoRa, GSM, power budgeting and devices that survive a field deployment.

PCB & Hardware Design

Schematic capture, layout, DFM, bring-up and debugging a board that arrived dead.

Embedded AI & Vision

Running inference on constrained silicon: quantisation, accelerators, camera pipelines.

Blockchain Hardware

Signing at the edge, key custody on device, and getting sensor data on-chain credibly.

CAD & Fabrication

Enclosures, tolerances, 3D printing and designing something you can actually manufacture.

SIWES & IT placements

Do your industrial training somewhere you actually build

We host SIWES and IT students from universities, polytechnics and colleges of education. A placement here is a bench, a project team and a supervisor — not a logbook you fill in at the end of the month.

Properly supervised

Logbooks signed, ITF documentation handled, and a supervisor who can actually assess the engineering you did.

On a real project

You join a project team with a deliverable and a deadline, and leave with something you can demonstrate at your defence.

The full curriculum

Placement students sit the same tiers our members do — embedded systems, robotics, IoT, PCB design, CAD, edge AI, 3D printing and fabrication.

First of its kind

The deep end

We are the first student-led hardware lab in South-East Nigeria to prototype in these areas. Alongside the technology programmes, placement students can work on the R&D lines we are building toward — long-horizon programmes where you would be joining early, on foundational work.

Electric motor manufacturing

Winding, stator and rotor design, controller electronics and test rigs — the part of the electrification supply chain Nigeria currently imports wholesale.

Manned aircraft prototyping

Airframe prototyping, avionics and control systems, working toward manned aircraft manufacturing — the longest horizon on our roadmap, and the reason the curriculum goes as deep as it does.

Microchip technology

From FPGA and CPU architecture through to silicon design — the foundation under every other programme we run.

Membership

Three levels, and the first one costs nothing

Membership is about access and commitment, not fees. You move up by building, and every level above Associate is earned rather than bought.

Associate

Free

Anyone enrolled at any institution in South-East Nigeria.

  • Community channel and study groups
  • Open build nights
  • Foundational tier (Modules 01–04)
  • Demo day attendance
Most common

Builder

Free · by cohort

Members who have cleared the foundational tier.

  • Everything in Associate
  • Full bootcamp seat
  • Component library borrowing privileges
  • Assigned mentor
  • Intermediate & advanced tiers
  • Project team placement

Resident

By application

Members carrying a capstone toward a real product.

  • Everything in Builder
  • Dedicated bench space
  • Budget for parts & fabrication
  • Incubation support: IP, pitch, pilot users
  • Industry placement & introductions
Free for now. Every level above is free of charge — membership, bootcamp seats and lab access. If that ever changes we will say so here first, and in the community channel, well before it takes effect.
